AFCCoach Development
Start hereHow the Studio worksThe Aspire method, from weekly theme to the field

Aspire sessions all follow one grammar. Learn it once and every week gets faster to plan — and every coach teaches the same football.

Self-paced chapters, with a Method-Ready badge at the end.

1
Set the week’s theme

Pick the game problem (moment → phase → situation) and the desired behavior. AFC suggests the principles, concepts and habit — edit anything.

2
Coordinate the microcycle

The Microcycle Map spreads that one theme across your training days — sweeping from the universal concept early to its final-third application by the weekend.

3
Build each session as a progression

Every session climbs Activation → Basic Situation → Extended Situation → Training Game → Cool-down: small and isolated to big and game-like.

4
Coach each drill the Aspire way

Every block carries a Target, Drill Dynamics, Rules and Coaching points by moment — plus a Delivery Guide (let them play, question, then add the points), Mistakes & Feedback, and Regression / Progression.

5
Deliver, reflect & share

Mark the session delivered, capture a quick reflection, and publish the week to the club — or export it as a Coach’s Playbook PDF.
